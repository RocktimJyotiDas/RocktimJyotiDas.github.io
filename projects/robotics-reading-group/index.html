About

An informal weekly reading group for graduate students interested in robot learning — manipulation, humanoid control, visuomotor policies, world models, and robotic foundation models. For the Fall 2026 semester, we are planning to focus more on robotic manipulation. Each week one person presents one or two recent papers and we discuss them together. Anyone is welcome to attend, and anyone can sign up to present.

Want to present? Pick a Tuesday from the schedule below and email Rocktim. Open slots are marked TBD.
